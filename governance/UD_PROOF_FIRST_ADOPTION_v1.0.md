# Proof-first UD research adoption v1.0 — MQM

Date: 2026-10-31. Scope: UD mathematical claims and their MQM research dependencies.
Canonical policy (proposed): https://github.com/ud-tetra/UD/blob/2a19727f15e038f39721088b960cd46b97fd9bc5/docs/UD_PROOF_FIRST_RESEARCH_GOVERNANCE_v1.0.md
Canonical governance PR: https://github.com/ud-tetra/UD/pull/473

Scientific progress is mathematical theorem/lemma closure, scoped no-go, and exact correction with source-bound proof and typed assumptions. Device demonstrations, resource comparisons and empirical MQM performance are distinct evidence objects. Code, CI and website releases are ancillary reproducibility mechanisms, not proofs of UD physics.

For any MQM result that is asserted as a UD mathematical theorem: create a theorem-quality proof and immutable proof ledger/receipt, preserving claim dependencies, exact source version, verification, counterexamples and physical-promotion status. Use the canonical UD proof archive as the program index after reviewed integration; local MQM research capture requirements remain fully in force. Never rewrite historical releases or invent hardware correspondence. Physical promotion 0. Review deferred, not waived.

This note is staged on an open PR; it does not yet amend merged main instructions or the live MQM site.

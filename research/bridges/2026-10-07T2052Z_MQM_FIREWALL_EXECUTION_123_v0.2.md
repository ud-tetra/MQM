2. prepare syndrome ancilla \(a\) in \(Z\) basis;

5. insert flag-to-syndrome CNOTs after data interactions 1 and 3;
6. measure syndrome in \(Z\), flag in \(X\);
7. undo the data basis rotations.

\[

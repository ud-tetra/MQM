# MQM canonical transition generators v0.1

The exact C2 x S4 x S4 factorization admits a five-generator factor-wise presentation: one central order-2 element, an order-2/order-3 pair for the protected-logical kernel S4 factor, and an order-2/order-3 pair for the second S4 factor.

Minimum abstract compile costs under the frozen H/S plus swap model are respectively: (9 local,0 swap), (0,1), (6,2), (9,1), and (6,2). The first S4 pair is protected-logically trivial. The second pair generates the nontrivial S3 logical quotient.

These are exact abstract compile counts, not physical timing or hardware performance. Physical promotion remains 0.

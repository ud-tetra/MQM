\section{Retained third commutator}
Let $H=H_B+K$ on the finite source sectors, $C=[K,H_B]$, $D=[C,H_B]$, and $\ad_A X=[A,X]$. The finite response is $\Delta_{2,M}(t)=\Tr_B X(t)$, with $\|X(t)\|_1\le2\nu$. Here $\nu$ is the finite reference occupation trace, not a continuum photon-number bound. Bath-only outer commutators vanish under the partial trace. Jacobi gives the exact identity
\begin{align}

\end{align}
For example, $\ad_{H_B}\ad_KX=\ad_K\ad_{H_B}X-\ad_CX$; moving the bath commutator outward under the trace generates the mixed terms in (\ref{third}). No assumption of commuting interaction-picture operators is made.

The inherited local bound is $\|K\|<14$. With $G^2=128/\pi$, the flat-band second and fourth moments are
\begin{equation}\|\delta c\|^2=G^2B^2/3,\qquad \|\delta^2c\|^2=G^2B^4/5.\end{equation}
Gauss nodes reproduce these polynomial moments. On $Q\le2$, excitation exchange has norm at most $\sqrt2$ times its one-particle coefficient norm. Thus, using $\pi>3$,
\begin{equation}\|C\|<256/3,\qquad \|D\|\le4096/\sqrt{5\pi}<4096/3.\end{equation}

&\le\nu\left(8\cdot14^3+12\cdot14\cdot\tfrac{256}{3}+2\cdot\tfrac{4096}{3}\right)\nonumber\\

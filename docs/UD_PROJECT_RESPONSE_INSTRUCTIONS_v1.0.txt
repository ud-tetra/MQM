MATHEMATICAL GOVERNANCE
* Define the object, domain, codomain, assumptions, conventions, and claimed conclusion before deriving a consequential result.
* Derive from source-bound variables. Never reverse engineer a target and present the result as an independent derivation. Label fitting, calibration, and inverse problems explicitly.
* Use exact fractions and symbolic expressions first; use decimals for measurements, numerical estimates, or readability with stated precision.
* Use prime factorization and clock/modular arithmetic when they expose a defined structural question. Numerical coincidences alone establish no mechanism or correspondence.
* Intuition, analogy, and guessing may generate hypotheses. Proof requires a valid argument; computed cases require reproducible evidence and remain scoped to those cases.
* Seek counterexamples, degeneracies, limiting cases, and assumption failures. For central identities and no-go claims, use an independent check when feasible.
* Track dimensions, units, metric choices, signs, orientations, scaling, and normalization. Keep unresolved choices OPEN_GATED; do not conceal them through rescaling or notation.
* AUTO_LOCK results only after their scoped proof or verification passes. Record assumptions, dependencies, verification method, and version. Recommendation alone cannot promote a candidate to a theorem.
* AUTO_FREEZE unsupported, falsified, source-blocked, or improperly reused claims. Record the reason and reopening conditions; preserve narrower valid results.
* State no-go results with their exact hypotheses. A changed assumption creates a new branch; it does not invalidate the original obstruction.

DYNAMICS AND PHYSICAL CORRESPONDENCE
* Specify the state space, evolution rule or generator, initial and boundary conditions, conserved quantities, coupling, and readout before claiming dynamical behavior.
* Distinguish algebraic agreement, dynamical persistence, and growth or cluster preference. Define the observable and comparison needed for each; do not infer one from another.

* Physical promotion remains 0 unless a specific correspondence passes its declared independent bridge test. Record any promotion only for that correspondence and evidence scope.

* Prefer target-blind preregistration before held-out tests. Freeze hypotheses, predictors, thresholds, exclusions, and interpretation rules before target exposure.
* After exposure, disclose amendments and classify reanalysis as exploratory unless fresh independent evaluation supports a renewed confirmatory claim.
* Report negative results, uncertainty, and unresolved alternatives. Simulation or agreement with a known number alone establishes no physical law.
